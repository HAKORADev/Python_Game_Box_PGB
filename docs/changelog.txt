v1.3.8 — May 2026
================================================================================

  New Game:
  - Pop TD — Tower Defense game with 6 tower types (Sharp, Cannon, Frost,
    Laser, Missile, Plasma), 15 enemy waves, upgrade system, sell towers,
    GPU-accelerated rendering via OpenGL, and procedural sound effects.

  Notes:
  - Pop TD uses OpenGL for GPU-accelerated rendering where available, with
    automatic CPU fallback.
  - Additional dependency: PyOpenGL (optional, for GPU rendering path).
  - All 13 games from v1.3.5 are carried forward unchanged.


================================================================================
v1.3.5 — March 14-15, 2026
================================================================================

  New Games:
  - Cosmic Spud — Survival shooter with auto-fire mechanics, wave-based enemy
    spawning with increasing difficulty, 10 unique enemy types (Basic, Fast,
    Tank, Ranged, Shooter, Aura, Spawner, Minion, Shielded, Healer), 10
    upgrade options with rarity system (Common, Rare, Epic), WASD/Arrow
    movement with mouse aiming, level-up system with permanent stat upgrades,
    critical hits, piercing shots, and multi-shot abilities.
  - Fruit Slasher — Swipe to slice fruits with mouse, avoid bombs (instant
    game over), combo system for multiple slices, 3 lives system, particle
    juice effects on slices, fruit halves fall with physics, high score
    tracking, start screen and game over screen.

  Game Changes (v1.1.1 → v1.3.5):

  Matcher:
  - 5 gem colors with cascade effects
  - 4 Special Powers: Matcher (auto-swap), Cleaner (clear row/col),
    Bomber (4x4 area), Vanisher (clear all of one color)
  - Round-based progression with increasing difficulty
  - Timer and move counter
  - Particle effects and smooth animations

  2048:
  - VS Player Mode with split board design (P1: WASD, P2: Arrow keys)
  - Particle effects on tile merges
  - Animated tile movements
  - Visual enhancements for tiles and transitions

  Dario:
  - 2 Players Mode (P2: A/D move, W jump)
  - Shared camera tracking both players
  - 5 Platform types: Static, Moving, Disappearing, Boost
  - 5 Power-Up types: Invincibility, Magnet, Slowmo, Score Multiplier,
    Gravity Reversal
  - Flying and rolling enemies
  - Particle effects and animations

  Escape The Maze:
  - Path Finder Power-Up (shows 5 steps ahead for 10 seconds, earned every
    2 wins, activated with Enter)
  - Enemy types: Red (chasing) and White (shooting)
  - Bullet dodging mechanics
  - Timer-based gameplay
  - Maze generation enhanced with dynamic scaling

  Geometry Flash:
  - Moving black obstacle squares (right to left, varied speeds)
  - Speed multiplier that increases over time
  - Coins for scoring
  - Pusher platforms that carry the player
  - Menu system with instructions
  - Smoothed acceleration & deceleration

  Hen Invaders:
  - 2 Players Mode (P2: A/D move, W shoot, E rockets)
  - AI Ship Power-Up: helps kill hens for 3 waves (press C)
  - Power-Up spawns increased (2-10 instead of 1-5)
  - Weapon upgrade system (spread shots, rockets)
  - Enhanced chicken movement patterns
  - Faster weapon response

  Keyboard Singer:
  - 10 Instrument Banks: Xylophone, Piano, Electric Guitar, BeatBox, Organ,
    Flute, Violin, Trumpet, Bass, Synth
  - Adjustable melody length in Free Play Mode (LAlt+RAlt)
  - Key Tapper Mode — type words against the clock
  - Longoly Mode — sustained notes while holding keys
  - Random melody generator (TAB key)
  - Reduced input lag, improved audio syncing

  Pong:
  - 3 AI difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard)
  - Ball trail effect
  - Particle background
  - Color-changing dynamic background
  - Enhanced ball physics

  Snake:
  - VS AI Mode with 3 difficulty levels (A* pathfinding)
  - 2 Players Mode (P2: WASD, Yellow Snake)
  - Black square hazard (touch = game over)
  - Speed increases x1.1 every 10 points
  - Particle effects, ripple animations, glowing visuals

  Snowy Tower:
  - VS AI (Easy/Medium/Hard) and VS Player modes (P2: A/D)
  - 5 Platform types: Static, Moving, Blinking, Disappearing,
    Moving-Blinking
  - Revamped camera system
  - Snow particles and cloud effects
  - Gradient backgrounds

  XO (Tic Tac Toe):
  - 4 AI difficulties: Easy, Medium, Hard, Overkill (perfect minimax)
  - AI taunts and messages
  - Sound effects and background music
  - Particle effects on wins
  - Score tracking across games

  Technical Improvements:
  - All games use PyQt5 or Pygame for consistent rendering
  - Pymunk physics engine for realistic particle effects
  - Pyganim for smooth sprite animations
  - Generated sound effects using numpy (no external audio files needed)
  - Consistent 60 FPS across all games
  - Dynamic backgrounds and visual effects
  - Responsive controls with minimal input lag
  - Now runs on Linux (in addition to Windows)
  - Source code released on GitHub (previous versions were exe-only)

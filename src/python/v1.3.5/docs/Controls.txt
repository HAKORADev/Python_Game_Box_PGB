### **Dario**
#### *Single Player Mode*:
- **Left & Right Arrows**: Movement
- **Space**: Jump

#### *Two Players Mode*:
- **Player 1**: **A, D** - Movement | **W** - Jump
- **Player 2**: **Left & Right Arrows** - Movement | **Space** - Jump

#### *When Lose*:
- **R**: Restart
- **ESC**: Exit

### **Keyboard Singer**
#### *Free Play Mode*:
- **0-9**: Different melody banks (Piano, Electric Guitar, BeatBox, Organ, Flute, Violin, Trumpet, Bass, Synth, Xylophone)
- **A-Z**: Play notes and build sequence
- **LCTRL**: Decrease melody weight
- **LSHIFT**: Increase melody weight
- **RCTRL**: Increase melody delay
- **RSHIFT**: Decrease melody delay
- **LALT**: Decrease melody length
- **RALT**: Increase melody length
- **TAB**: Generate random melody sequence
- **Enter**: Play the built sequence
- **Space**: Toggle Autoplay on/off
- **Backspace** (when Autoplay is On): Toggle Longoly mode (sustained notes)
- **ESC**: Return to menu

#### *Key Tapper Mode*:
- **A-Z**: Type letters to match the word
- **Backspace**: Delete last character
- **Enter**: Submit answer

### **Pong**
#### *VS AI Mode*:
- **W, S**: Movement (left paddle)
- Select AI difficulty: **1** (Easy), **2** (Medium), **3** (Hard)
- **ESC**: Return to menu

#### *VS Player Mode*:
- **Player 1**: **W, S** - Movement (left paddle)

### **Matcher**
- **Mouse**: Click and drag to swap adjacent gems
- Match 3 or more gems of the same color
- **Power-Ups**: Click on icons at bottom to activate:
  - **Matcher**: Auto-swaps gems to create matches
  - **Cleaner**: Clears a random row or column
  - **Bomber**: Clears a 4x4 area
  - **Vanisher**: Clears all gems of one color

#### *Game Over*:
- **R**: Restart
- **ESC**: Exit

---

### **Fruit Slasher**
- **Mouse**: Swipe/drag to slice fruits
- Avoid slicing bombs!
- Don't miss more than 3 fruits

### **Cosmic Spud**
- **W, A, S, D** or **Arrow Keys**: Movement
- **Mouse**: Aim direction
- Auto-shoot enabled
- Collect XP gems, coins, and health pickups
- Level up to choose upgrades

#### *Game Over*:
- **R**: Restart
- **ESC**: Exit
